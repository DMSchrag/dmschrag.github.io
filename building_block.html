Designing a Building Block

Here is the iteration process for my building-block design.

Design 1

The general idea for a building block was to be quite literally and take the idea of a block, of something square that is already preshaped. So the first thing I did was to sketch such a simple cube.

Design 2

For the second sketch I let my mind wander a bit, just the tiniest step and thought that a builing block was something that don't necessarily needed to be a cube, so one could maybe also make it longer? Change the side length of our previous cube?

Design 3

It started to dawn on me that I could make any building block, that anything I could think of as long I build something out of it is a building block, so I wanted it to be able to have wheels as well! The Idea was to have a lot of bricks with holes that one can interconnect to make a base where on the sides one could also attach wheels!

Design 4

But so if I wanted the blocks to connect with each other how would I do that? So V4, V5, V6 and V7 were drawn. In V4 the idea was to have a chunky side strip but on furhter thought this would just now work, it can not interconnect with other building blocks. In V5 the idea was to have two blocks and a middle piece that could be pushed inbetween them and then interconnect the building blocks! But this seemed quite tidious and not so fun in the long run. So I took a look at the frontrunnter of blocks, "Lego" and thoughz about a similiar snapping system by having the exact match of plug and hole. But then what if I wanted no plugs on the side? Yet I wanted the possibility to maybe plug something into from the side so V6 was born but going back into the concept of having a third piece to interconnect the building blocks. This all didn't work out as I wanted and I felt frustration because my blocks felt as if they were just copying other blocks that already exist. So I left my drawing board for a few days to recharge.

Design 5

So after sleeping over it for a day, I redebated what I wanted and what I thought of as a building block, I started to look at it as something I can expand on, which could have any size, shape or form as long as I can build upon it. So since I still wanted to make a car I thought, I can just make a baseplate with holes so that I can plug other building blocks in it!

Design 6

I was still not happy with just a plate as a building block design so it suddendly hit me, I could actually build someting cool in this course! So I tried to think of a few different things I wanted to build but never really had the time, one was a custom gamepad for my mobile phone, clip-on skates for my shoes and a sunfollowing beam redirection device as well as a different build of a previouse airplane. And here crawled in the hype and realization I could try to make the fuselage my building block. So the main body of the airplane, and then having different wings be attacheable, I thought this would be a really fun and unique building block! So on the backsite one could have interchangeable motors, and on the site interchangeable wings, so I would need to tinker a system where I can switch them both out easily!

Design 7

This is the final sketch where I have holes on the back and on the site to then use clippers to attach and deattach the wings on the side as well as the motor!
